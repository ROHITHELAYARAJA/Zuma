# Research radar

Define the decision the research should support before collecting sources. Use Firecrawl, an available Apify connector, or other live research tools for current web facts. Prefer primary sources for rules, pricing, product capability, and technical claims. Record source links and dates, and note uncertainty or geographic limits.

Turn findings into a short decision memo: what changed, why it matters to Freelance, Career, Dev, Research, or Personal, and the next action. Deduplicate findings against prior reports when possible. Do not treat a scraped lead as a qualified prospect without validation.

For recurring monitoring, stay quiet when nothing meaningful changes. Notify on a material opportunity, deadline, competitor change, failure, or needed user action.
